How to Play

Sets: Low (A–6), High (8–K) per suit + 7s & Jokers = 9 sets total.

Ask: On your turn, click Ask under an opponent's name. Pick a card — but only from a set you already hold a card of. If they have it, you get it and ask again. If not, their turn begins.

Declare: Any player can declare a set anytime using the Declare button. Assign all 6 cards to your teammates.

✅ All correct → Your team wins the set.

❌ Wrong, card with opponent → Opponents win the set.

❌ Wrong, card within your team → Set is discarded.

Win: All 9 sets declared — most sets wins!
