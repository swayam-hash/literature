How to Play

Sets: Low (A–6), High (8–K) per suit, and 7s & Jokers — 9 sets total.

Ask: On your turn, ask any opponent for a card — but only if you already hold a card from the same set. If they have it, you get it and ask again. If not, their turn begins.

Declare: Any player can declare a set anytime. Assign all 6 cards of a set to your teammates.

✅ All correct → Your team wins the set.

❌ Wrong, card with opponent → Opponents win the set.

❌ Wrong, card within your team → Set is discarded.

Win: All 9 sets declared. Most sets wins!
